WHO WE ARE

A helping hand. A fresh start.

AASRA Foundation is a welfare and rehabilitation initiative envisioned to support homeless people, vulnerable families and individuals experiencing difficult circumstances in Lahore.

Our approach is rooted in compassion, safety, respect and practical support that helps people work towards a more stable and independent future.

✓Respect for every individual
✓Safe and inclusive support
✓A path towards independence
