WHO WE ARE

A helping hand. A fresh start.

AASRA Foundation is a welfare initiative concept focused on the needs of vulnerable individuals, people experiencing homelessness and families facing difficult circumstances in Lahore.

The proposed approach combines compassion, practical assistance, community participation and pathways towards greater independence.

✓Respect for every individual
✓Safe and inclusive support
✓Transparency and accountability
✓Opportunities for independence
