OUR PURPOSE

A community built on care and dignity.

AASRA Foundation is a welfare initiative concept focused on vulnerable individuals, people experiencing homelessness and families facing difficult circumstances in Lahore.

The aim is to explore ways to connect people with appropriate resources, community support and opportunities for greater independence.

✓ Our Mission: Put human dignity first
✓ Our Vision: A more supportive community
✓ Our Values: Respect, fairness and transparency
